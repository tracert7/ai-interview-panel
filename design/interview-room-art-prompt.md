Prompt: Create a clean 4:3 landscape pixel-art interview room background for a working Korean interview-practice web app. Use the attached reference only for its warm office palette, crisp 16-bit pixel-art aesthetic and slightly elevated front/top-down camera. Show exactly three distinct adult interviewers seated behind one long wooden desk across the upper-middle of the room: left a dark-haired person in a navy suit, center a gray-haired person with glasses and a burgundy tie, right a brown-haired person in a dark suit. Show one candidate viewed from behind in a chair near the bottom center. Preserve generous space between characters. Include a window and leafy potted plant on the left, a wall clock and blank dark wall display behind the panel, a small bookcase on the right, warm gray-beige tiles, paper and small desk microphones. Crisp square pixels, tidy game-background composition, soft daylight, muted brown wood and navy accents. The whole image is the room scene only, with no contact sheet, no character lineup, no UI panels, no speech bubbles, no written text, no labels, no logos or watermark. The app will add all labels and interactive dialogue as live HTML. The room should feel calm, professional and welcoming rather than fantasy or combat. Characters must remain clearly visible at small display sizes.
